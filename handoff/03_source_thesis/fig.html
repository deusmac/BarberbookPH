Technology Acceptance Model

Davis (1989); Venkatesh et al. (2003)

Perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use influence the intention to use a system.

DeLone and McLean IS Success Model

DeLone and McLean (2003)

System and information quality lead to use, user satisfaction, and net benefits.

Psychology of Waiting Lines

Maister (1985); Taylor (1994)

Uncertain and unexplained waits feel longer and lower the evaluation of the service.

ISO/IEC 25010 Quality Model

ISO (2011, 2023)

Functionality, usability, efficiency, reliability, and satisfaction as measures of software quality.

BarberBook PHOnline Appointment and Haircut Preference System for Local Barbershops

INPUT

Knowledge Requirements
  • Existing appointment and haircut preference practices
  • Problems of customers and barbers
  • Needed system features
Software Requirements
  • HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap
  • PHP (Laravel), MySQL
  • XAMPP, VS Code, GitHub, Figma
Hardware Requirements
  • Laptop/desktop (8 GB RAM or higher)
  • Android / iOS phone
  • Stable internet connection

PROCESS

Agile Software Development
  • Planning and requirements gathering (interview, observation, survey)
  • Design (DFD, ERD, use case, screen layout)
  • Development by sprint
    Sprint 1: accounts and booking
    Sprint 2: gallery and preferences
    Sprint 3: dashboard and notifications
  • Testing
  • Deployment
Evaluation
  • ISO/IEC 25010-based questionnaire
  • Weighted mean

OUTPUT

BarberBook PH: An Online Appointment and Haircut Preference System for Local Barbershops

Acceptability results in terms of functionality, usability, efficiency, reliability, and user satisfaction
